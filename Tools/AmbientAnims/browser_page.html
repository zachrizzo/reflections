Ambient Animation Library

Everyday town life for the NPCs of Reflections: work, talk, rest, drink and pray. Every clip was generated with Kimodo from the sentences shown on its card, on a MetaHuman, and baked in place. The figures replay each clip's real joint motion; blue pencil is the body's left side.

Using these clips

Loops repeat an activity. Most end within a few centimetres of their first pose, so a 0.3 second crossfade back to the start hides the seam. Actions are one-off beats that begin and end near a relaxed stand.

Props (dashed chips) are mimed. The generator cannot see an axe or a mug and the body has no finger animation, so the prop attaches to the hand socket at runtime and some grips need a small offset.

Seated clips sit on an invisible seat at standard height: place the NPC on a bench or stool. Scores: energy is how much the hands, head and feet move, slide is how fast a planted foot skates in cm/s, seam is how far a loop's last pose is from its first in cm. A clip is flagged FROZEN? when its prompt asked for an action but the take barely moves; clips meant to be still (standing guard, dozing) say held pose instead. Redo queued means a better prompt is waiting for the next generation pass; the current take works as a placeholder.
