## Spatial hints found
- The sides are mirrored: the Frost Tower is the Gilt Tower's own reflection (same footprint, "same tower, colder light"). You step from inside the Gilt Tower up into the Frost Tower. The Weigh-House (Pewterside) is the third tower.
- Giltside: Lantern Gate has a bridge; the camp is under the gate; the canal rift post is a separate spot (Third Company pushes drifters "back through the canal rift"), so put a canal near the gate. Fish Steps "by the river" (Pewterside); Horace says someone watched the river from the Weigh-House roof, so the roof overlooks the river.
- Lamplight: Wren's start is outside the inn, facing the skyline; the cellar is under the inn; the inn is probably next to Bell Square (inferred: Goldie and Jory haunt it). Archive steps are where Lucan, Tobias and Odette meet; Lucan works at the Bell Square fountain, so Archive and Bell Square are probably close (inferred).
- Lantern Row: Ilse, Barnaby, Dov, Amos; Dov's furnace and Barnaby's shop are on or off the Row; Maud's key is "under a lamp that never goes out" (any lamp).
- Bell Lane: Pim, Tobin, Hollis and Nell hang here; Corin runs the lamps; Corin also drinks at the Lamplight (inferred: Lamplight is near Bell Lane).
- Pewterside: Coal Lane has the tram stop, Hester's doorstep and the laundry on one street; Fish Steps is a short walk away (Ada's haunt is the stop, Frank's is the steps); Wick Street infirmary is separate. Edie rides a tram line (the Coal Lane line).
- Frostside: the Long Shelter is the haunt of 6 people (Ice Stairs, Bone Market, shed folk) so it sits central; the Frozen Gate and Ice Wall are the outer edge; Sorrel's shed is near the Bone Market (haunt) and Petra's haunt is the Bone Market; Mk_Frost_Tower_FrostTower is above/at the Gilt Tower's position.
- Placement tolerance: unplaced Mk_ names fall back to the generic kind (Mk_<Side>_<Kind>), then a nav point 10-25 m from the side anchor, so only the named places matter.
