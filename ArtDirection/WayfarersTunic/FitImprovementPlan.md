# Wayfarer’s Tunic improvement plan

Keep one Unreal Wardrobe Item for all compatible bodies. Rebuild the tunic from a sewn garment pattern and use Epic’s stock outfit as a control to find out how much of the clipping comes from the animation. The first milestone is one tunic with the intended silhouette on unseen bodies and measured clearance through motion. The existing tunic at commit `ad2271be` is the comparison garment.

## What the measurements say

All 24 wearers passed the current standing waist, front-chest and shoulder checks. The measured extra waist circumference spans 3.9–5.9 cm. The front-chest check samples 27 rays. A complete chest circumference was measurable on only **2 of 24** wearers with the current ring method. A shirt can meet the current measurements and still trace the chest too closely, pull at the armhole, or have the wrong sleeve or collar shape. The generator cuts the body’s triangles and offsets them to form the garment. That construction is a plausible contributor to the appearance the user rejected. [Measurements](Review/FitSamples.json) · [Source garment construction](../../Tools/ClothingFit/build_wayfarers_tunic.py)

The cast accounts for 72% of confirmed moving contacts. For 23 wearers the worst sample was 22% into the cast. Across all moving samples, 87% of contacts fell within 20 mm of a bare-body self-intersection. That proximity identifies a hypothesis we can test. It cannot assign a cause to each contact. Counts are comparable for this baseline and depend on mesh density. [Fit scorecard](Review/FitScorecard.md)

## Experiments in order

1. **Run a control in Test Town.** Use BridgeGuard as an authoring reference, GiltBarnaby for the standing failure and GiltRamon for the worst cast. On each body, capture the underlying skin, the installed Epic `/MetaHumanCharacter/Optional/Clothing/WI_DefaultGarment`, and the current tunic separately. Compare overlapping *upper-body* regions. Play the full cast with identical timing through both the direct-clip preview and the gameplay animation path. The current harness seeks a clip on the body at three times. Record affected body parts, peak penetration in mm and duration in frames. If both garments fail at the same arm–torso contact, test the pose. A failure specific to the tunic goes to pattern or weighting. [Current capture code](../../Tools/ClothingFit/capture_lineup_motion.py)

2. **Build one actual tunic pattern.** Draft front, back and sleeve panels with a cut neckline, sewn shoulder and side seams, armholes and a finished hem. Drape it on one reference body under gravity. Measure shoulder seam placement, armhole depth, upper-arm ease, sleeve opening, hem flare and the depth of the front panel between the chest peaks. Define the intended values on the reference garment before scaling up. A side-by-side design review establishes whether it looks like the intended woven tunic. Record the corresponding dimensions so they can be reproduced. Use that same pattern to generate the four existing reference sizes. Inspect the draped source, imported source and Unreal-resized output separately to learn where a defect enters. Epic supports both a Marvelous Designer/CLO USD containing render and simulation meshes and a manually authored simulation mesh alongside a render mesh. Blender can sew panels with sewing springs. [Epic’s outfit authoring paths](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/building-an-outfit-asset-in-unreal-engine) · [Blender cloth sewing](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/physics/cloth/settings/shape.html)

3. **Test sleeve weights and arm movement independently.** With simulation off, compare the default skin-weight transfer with one shared underarm selection and weight correction on the garment. Inspect which bones pull each side of the armhole. Epic documents armpit tearing as a transfer failure and provides selection-based weight operations. For recurring bare-arm contacts, try the same cast on a copy of the retargeter with **Body Intersect Goals** off and on, using hand goals and torso collision bodies sized for the target. The experimental Spatially Aware Retarget Ops plugin ships in the installed UE 5.9. It adjusts selected goals, so also measure elbow and forearm clearance, hand trajectory and cast timing. Change one variable per run. [Epic’s underarm skinning workflow](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/building-a-metahuman-parametric-outfit-with-a-usd-export) · [Epic’s retarget operations](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/retargeting-operation-stack-in-unreal-engine-5-8)

4. **Add cloth motion where it improves the garment.** Compare the new sewn tunic skinned and simulated on the same body and pose. Start with a low-resolution simulation mesh, anchored neckline and shoulders, and limited travel at the sleeve ends and hem. Check body colliders at the short and broad extremes. Advance the solver continuously at a fixed step and record the final deformed surface. The current C++ reader computes bone-skinned LOD 0 positions. Validate the new measurement path by pinning a hem point, releasing it, and measuring the difference at a fixed body pose. Compare penetration, hem travel, return to rest, stretch and frame cost. Set a stretch limit from the intended fabric before tuning the solver. The existing 20% edge limit is a project working value. [Current surface reader](../../Source/ClothingFitEditor/ClothingFitLibrary.cpp) · [Epic’s Panel Cloth workflow](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/panel-cloth-editor-overview) · [Marvelous Designer’s strain measurement](https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/56518392287513--Fit-Maps-Strain-Map)

5. **Qualify the single Wardrobe Item.** Keep the current 24 as regression cases. Reserve a fresh body created after the pattern and settings are frozen for a blind fit. Run the complete walk, crouch and cast frame by frame, then run game transitions such as crouch-to-cast. Score each garment region against its design dimensions, record unmasked clipping depth and duration, and flag incomplete mask or surface data as unresolved. Repair the chest measurement by isolating the torso from the arms at consistent landmarks and reporting measurement coverage. Compare stretch at the shoulders and armholes separately from hem motion. Verify the selected gameplay LODs and a packaged build. Acceptance requires the intended silhouette, the recorded sizing limits, zero confirmed unmasked penetration beyond the documented measurement precision, resolved uncertain regions and performance within the project’s NPC budget. Every wearer uses the same item with zero wearer-specific garment edits. [Epic’s automatic sizing and masking workflow](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/testing-and-configuring-your-parametric-outfit-asset)

The **first deliverable** is a full-cast contact report for the three pilot bodies and a sewn tunic prototype draped on one reference body. Those results determine where to spend the next round of work before scaling to the lineup or adding garments.
