# Asset request 2 for Codex: mock city kit (from the Claude orchestrator), 2026-11-14 16:00

The mock city stands in for the coworker's real map until it lands, and world-jump owns it. It already plays with engine shapes plus your existing LanthorneAssets props, so everything below is an upgrade, **ordered by visual payoff**. Please do them in order and stop wherever time runs out.

Each item comes in three side variants unless noted:
- **Pewterside**: dull grey, everyday, gas-lit.
- **Giltside**: warm, gilded, lanterns.
- **Frostside**: cold, cracked, bleached.

All at real-world scale with the pivot at bottom centre. Import to `/Game/LanthorneAssets/<Side>/City/`, named `SM_<Side>_<Name>`; world-jump swaps them in.

1. **Facade kit**, modules about 4 m wide: a ground floor with door, an upper floor with windows, a corner, and a roof edge or cornice. They must tile side by side and stack, with flat sides and back. This replaces about 100 box buildings per side and is the biggest win.
2. **Street lamp post**, about 4 m tall: a Giltside lantern post, a Pewterside gas lamp, a Frostside brazier stand. Used about 100 times per side, so keep it light (under 5k triangles).
3. **Market stall**, about 3.5 × 2 × 2.6 m: a counter with a canopy.
4. **Canal pieces**: a 10 m bridge span with railings, plus a 4 m quay wall and parapet segment that tiles.
5. **City gate arch**, about 18 m wide and 11 m tall.
6. **Fountain** with an 8 m basin.
7. **Front door**, 1.6 × 2.5 m.
8. **Stair flight module**, 2.4 m wide, that tiles in height.
9. **Pewterside only**: a tram stop shelter and a tram rail section.
10. **Giltside only**: a drifter tent (about 3.4 m). **Frostside only**: a timber shed (4 × 4 × 3 m).
11. Optional and big, tower shells:
    - the Gilt Tower, 14 × 14 × 24 m, with a door opening;
    - the Frost Tower, sealed and cracked, with a 26 m crown platform;
    - the Pewterside Weigh-House, 15 × 15 × 10 m, with a stair round the back and a drainpipe.
