# Asset request 3 for Codex: FORM-FITTING clothing for every NPC (from the Claude orchestrator), 2026-11-14 18:25

The user, after playtesting: "Baggy clothes don't really work. They need to be form-fitting." Codex GENERATES the garments (image, then image-to-3D, as before). A Claude session then FITS them to each MetaHuman body in Blender (conform, skin weights, clipping checks). Nobody shapes garments by hand.

## What to generate
Each garment is a **separate mesh**, worn on an invisible standard adult body in an **A-pose**, close to the skin (fitted tailoring, no flowing or baggy cloth, no capes or loose mantles). Generate for a **male** and a **female** body unless noted. Each piece:
- has sensible thickness;
- has closed edges at the cuffs, collar and hem;
- has no body, hands or head included.

Per side, with the look and mood from the canon: Pewterside dull grey and everyday, Giltside warm and gilded, Frostside cold, pale and cracked-frost accents.

| Role | Upper | Lower | Feet |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| Civilian (worker) | fitted work shirt + fitted waistcoat or short jacket | fitted trousers (M) / fitted ankle skirt (F) | low boots |
| Civilian (merchant / artisan) | tailored coat to the hip, fitted | fitted trousers / fitted skirt | shoes |
| Guard | fitted padded jerkin with leather straps and a side emblem | fitted trousers with knee guards | tall boots |
| Soldier (Frostside enemy) | fitted armoured tunic, plates kept tight to the body | fitted leggings | armoured boots |
| Caster (Frostside enemy) | fitted high-collar robe-coat to the knee, slim, not flowing | fitted leggings | boots |

That's 5 roles × 3 sides × 2 sexes. Priority order if time runs short:
1. Giltside civilian (worker), then guard;
2. Frostside soldier, then caster;
3. Pewterside civilian;
4. everything else.
