## 2. Quality evidence (foot sliding, jitter, root motion, camera motion, occlusion, fast action, fingers, face, multi-person)

### Takeaway
There is no independent, measured, side-by-side comparison for 2026 versions. Evidence is vendor claims, a few forum anecdotes and trade-press hands-on notes, all pointing the same way: monocular tools give usable blocking and decent locomotion on clean, static-camera, single-person footage, but foot contact, hands, occlusion and fast action need cleanup, and the vendors themselves say so.

### Cited Findings
- Rokoko (a hardware seller, Oct 2025) says monocular AI "isn't perfect at capturing nuanced contact with the floor" and that "Some struggle with occlusions"; it says "none have fully solved global accuracy" or multi-character interaction. — [Rokoko insights 2025-10-29](https://www.rokoko.com/insights/the-future-of-motion-capture)
- Rokoko Vision 3.0 launched "without finger tracking" and a foot-lock filter exists in Rokoko Studio for cleanup; Rokoko's own FAQ says Smartgloves remain most accurate for fingers. Root motion, occlusion and multi-person behaviour of Vision 3.0 are not described. — [Vision 3.0 post](https://www.rokoko.com/insights/introducing-rokoko-vision-3-0); [Vision page](https://www.rokoko.com/ja/products/vision)
- QuickMagic user on Reallusion's forum: "There was a bit of jitter in the mocap" (fixed by curve smoothing); "Move.ai may have a bit better mocap quality but are much more expensive"; shoulders raised. One anecdote on a vendor-run forum. — [Reallusion forum](https://discussions.reallusion.com/t/first-full-body-mocap-test-using-quickmagic-and-accuface/15520)
- QuickMagic says static and moving cameras are supported "for selected workflows" and that camera shake and rapid viewpoint changes reduce stability. — [QuickMagic home](https://www.quickmagic.ai)
- iClone Video Mocap (QuickMagic-based): Reallusion advises a static eye-level camera; tracking may fail if the subject leaves frame or motion is rapid or occluded; foot slippage is cleaned in iClone; finger tracking included, quality not assessed; facial capture is a separate paid add-on. — [Digital Production 2025-11-03](https://digitalproduction.com/2025/11/03/video-mocap-reallusions-ai-gets-moving/)
- Move One guidance (via search summary): fixed camera, one centred performer, Full HD, at least 60 fps recommended; paid Move Pro tier said to have markedly better foot contact. Not opened on a primary page. — [search results, SNIPPET](https://docs.move.ai/knowledge/move-one)
- MoCap Online (sells mocap packs, so biased) claims Move.ai gets "roughly 85 to 90 percent" of optical positional accuracy for locomotion, with a wider gap for fast or occluded motion, hand tracking "notoriously difficult", and Radical single-camera accuracy "lags". No method given. — [MoCap Online](https://mocaponline.com/blogs/mocap-news/ai-game-animation-tools-2026)
- Epic markerless plugin: Digital Production states "we have not had accuracy metrics, solve times, cleanup effort, camera movement limits" and positions it for blocking, previs and indie capture. One forum user: "As a first release results are pretty good!"; another reports 29.97/30 fps footage "do not work" (unconfirmed fix: check frame-rate metadata); a user-suggested multi-person workaround is not official; install errors (MD-0011-0) reported. — [Digital Production](https://digitalproduction.com/2026/06/23/metahuman-gets-markerless-mocap/); [Epic forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/epic-games-metahuman-animator-markerless-motion-capture-plugin/2729293)
- Cascadeur's own docs-level claim: video mocap since 2023; 2025.2 "automatically improves global positioning and cleans foot sliding." One trade source (Film Threat) says Cascadeur does not extract motion from video, contradicting Cascadeur's feature list. — [search results, SNIPPET](https://cascadeur.com/blog/general/cascadeur-20231-introduces-finger-autoposing-video-mocap-and-macos-version); [Film Threat](https://filmthreat.com/features/10-best-ai-tools-for-turning-video-into-3d-character-motion/)
- NVIDIA GEM-X model card: handles "dynamic cameras", outputs world-space global trajectories, 77-joint SOMA body; RGB pipeline tracks one person; hands via a separate gated checkpoint. Research claims, no independent test found. — [model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/GEM-X/blob/main/README.md)
- DeepMotion offers foot-locking modes (Auto/Always/Never/Grounding) per a search summary; its multi-person beta (up to 8 people, Oct 2023) and Unreal root-joint options are documented in its own release notes. — [DeepMotion release updates](https://deepmotion.com/release-updates)

### Inferences
- For a game, the practical bar is "blocking quality plus cleanup in Sequencer/Control Rig/IK"; none of the sources support using raw output unreviewed.
- Root motion and world-space translation are the least-documented dimension for every vendor except GEM-X (claimed) and Cascadeur (claimed); this needs a test clip, not more reading.

### Gaps
- No Reddit/r/unrealengine/r/gamedev or YouTube comparisons retrievable; no CGPress/80.lv hands-on of Vision 3.0, QuickMagic, Plask, Flow Studio mocap or the Epic plugin with measured foot-slide.
- Nothing on fast action (combat, falls, rolls) beyond generic caveats.
- Face: Flow Studio sends face tracking via Blender scene export; Epic plugin can solve face and body from one video (via MetaHuman Animator); no quality evidence.

## 5. Local vs cloud, retention, training on uploads, and API for batch automation

### Takeaway
Every vendor product except the Epic plugin, GEM-X and Cascadeur desktop is cloud: the video is uploaded. Several terms let the vendor train on uploads (Move One, Uthana even for paying customers, Rokoko in anonymized form, DeepMotion to "improve" services). QuickMagic and Reallusion promise not to train on private uploads. Local options avoid the upload question entirely.

### Cited Findings
- **Epic plugin:** "Everything runs on your own machine. Your footage never leaves your workstation." No cloud or credits; Python and Blueprint automation; Take Archive Ingest suggested for bulk import. — [Epic forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/epic-games-metahuman-animator-markerless-motion-capture-plugin/2729293)
- **Move One:** cloud; licence to Move AI for "the development, training and improvement of our products" (App EULA §7.2); API exists ("Move One API EULA" listed). — [EULA](https://www.move.ai/move-one-app-eula); [terms index](https://www.move.ai/terms-of-use)
- **Rokoko:** cloud; Rokoko may use User Content "to improve the Services" and "sub-license this to third parties in an anonymized form" (§3.4); §6.3 says User Content is "fully anonymized and never distributed in its original form"; storage 1 month on Starter, unlimited on Basic. No public API found. — [Terms v2.0.1](https://www.rokoko.com/studio-term-of-use); [Vision page](https://www.rokoko.com/products/vision)
- **DeepMotion:** cloud; licence to "Process uploaded Content for the sole purpose of improving the Site and services" (USER UPLOADED CONTENT); uploads "private to you"; API only "reach out to us about our API plans". — [Terms (Mar 30, 2025)](https://www.deepmotion.com/terms-of-use); [FAQ](https://www.deepmotion.com/faq)
- **QuickMagic:** cloud; §4: "we will not use your private uploaded content to train general-purpose AI models" unless authorized; free assets stored 15 days; API via a separate plan (/consult), "Studio does not include API access". — [Agreement](https://docs.quickmagic.ai/docs/agreement.html); [Pricing](https://www.quickmagic.ai/Pricing)
- **Uthana:** cloud; §6.2 licence to use "eligible Customer Inputs" for training and "Payment alone does not create a no-training arrangement"; deleted content excluded from future training within 30 days (§9.1); GraphQL API shares credits with app. — [Terms v2.0.0](https://uthana.com/terms); [Pricing](https://uthana.com/pricing)
- **Reallusion:** cloud; §5.4 Private Content not used for training without "explicit prior written consent"; Free-plan output is "Public by Default" (§5.1) and public content may be used to train (§5.3). — [AI Service EULA](https://www.reallusion.com/Content/EULA/AI-Service.htm)
- **Plask:** cloud; Terms (2023) let Plask use posted content "for any purpose, including for advertising" and process User IP "through machine learning"; "Build with the Plask API" link exists. — [Plask Terms](https://plask.ai/docs/100002)
- **Autodesk Flow Studio:** cloud; General Terms (search snippet): Autodesk personnel will not use Your Content except at request/consent or "in connection with providing and improving Offerings"; AI-training stance for outputs not found. — [search result, SNIPPET](https://www.autodesk.com/company/terms-of-use/en/special-terms)
- **Viggle:** cloud; terms page 404 to the fetch tool; secondary reviews claim free-tier content can be used for training: UNVERIFIED. PINOC "runs behind the Viggle API". — [PINOC](https://viggle.ai/pinoc/ai-motion-capture)
- **GEM-X:** local; hardware validated on A100 80 GB; "advises users to have rights to their input content". — [model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/GEM-X/blob/main/README.md)

### Inferences
- If the source clips are third-party (YouTube), a local tool removes the vendor-upload representation problem entirely; it does not remove the separate question of whether using the performance is allowed (see section 7).
- Only Uthana (GraphQL), Move One (API EULA), QuickMagic (API plan), Viggle and DeepMotion (on request) clearly offer automation; Epic's plugin offers local Python/Blueprint automation.

### Gaps
- Retention periods for most vendors; Autodesk and Move training stance on Output; Rokoko API.

## 6. Export formats and the Unreal / MetaHuman path

### Takeaway
Most services export FBX (often with Mixamo, UE5 Manny or HumanIK rigs) that you must retarget to `metahuman_base_skel` with an IK Retargeter. Only the Epic plugin is MetaHuman-native; QuickMagic advertises a "UE MetaHuman" export and Flow Studio claims MetaHuman-compatible data.

## 7. Commercial terms (ownership, commercial use per tier, uploaded-video rights, training, redistribution)

### Takeaway
Terms differ sharply. Clearest for a sold game: Uthana (paying customers own outputs, may use them in games and sell them as asset packs) and QuickMagic (paid plans "support commercial use", free does not). DeepMotion allows paid-plan output inside a game as "binary only" but forbids stand-alone distribution and retargeted resale. Move One's EULA keeps IP in the Output with Move AI and licenses it back, never mentions games, and permits training on your uploads. Rokoko's terms are silent on output commercial rights. All cloud vendors push responsibility for upload rights to you, which conflicts with using third-party YouTube footage. The Epic plugin is local and described as free for commercial use, but its licence text was not found.

Shooting your own reference clips, or hiring performers under a release, avoids both the vendor representation and the YouTube download problem.

## 8. Fit for a MetaHuman cast (UE 5.9, `metahuman_base_skel`) and whether a video-to-animation pipeline is worth building

### Takeaway
For this project the most promising path is Epic's free local MetaHuman Animator markerless plugin (no upload, MetaHuman-native skeleton, scriptable), provided it works in 5.9 and the quality meets a test. Cloud tools are fallbacks and need FBX retargeting; commercial terms are best at Uthana and QuickMagic (paid). None of these solves the legal problem of using third-party YouTube performances.

### Cited Findings
- Epic plugin: body with hands, face optional from the same video, no retargeting between MetaHumans, Python/Blueprint automation, Windows only, UE 5.8+ and experimental (no 5.9 statement found). — [Epic docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-animation-from-mono-video-capture-in-unreal-engine); [Digital Production](https://digitalproduction.com/2026/06/23/metahuman-gets-markerless-mocap/)
- Reported Epic plugin issues: 29.97/30 fps footage failure reports, install errors, processing "might be very long"; Epic recommends a 16-core, 32 GB machine. — [Epic forum](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/epic-games-metahuman-animator-markerless-motion-capture-plugin/2729293)
- Cloud candidates with UE or Manny-style skeletons: Rokoko Vision (UE5 Manny skeleton, FBX free), QuickMagic (UE5.5/5.6 and "UE MetaHuman" presets), Move One (MetaHuman retargeting tutorials), Flow Studio (MetaHuman body claim). — [Rokoko](https://www.rokoko.com/products/vision); [QuickMagic](https://www.quickmagic.ai); [Move docs](https://docs.move.ai/knowledge/move-one); [Autodesk (SNIPPET)](https://www.autodesk.com/products/flow-studio/features)
- Rokoko's Vision 3.0 has no finger tracking; DeepMotion/QuickMagic/iClone/Viggle/Plask advertise hand or finger data (quality unassessed). — [Vision 3.0 post](https://www.rokoko.com/insights/introducing-rokoko-vision-3-0); [Plask](https://plask.ai/pricing); [Viggle](https://viggle.ai/pinoc/ai-motion-capture)

### Inferences
- A first test should use the user's own 10 s reference clips through (a) the Epic plugin and (b) one paid cloud tool with clean terms (Uthana or QuickMagic Pro), then compare foot contact, root travel and hands in the project; this costs under about USD 60.
- Avoid building on Move One (acquisition limbo, training clause, silent on games), Plask (2023 non-commercial terms), Radical and Meshcapade (gone), or any free tier.
- A bulk "100 YouTube clips" pipeline is the highest-risk variant (vendor terms, YouTube terms, possible copyright and likeness issues); a 10-to-20 clip pipeline from self-shot or licensed footage is low risk and cheap.
- Because the character skeleton is custom, the retargeting step (IK Retargeter plus Control Rig cleanup) is the hidden cost of every FBX route.

### Gaps
- No UE 5.9 compatibility statement or hands-on quality test for the Epic plugin; the owner's own test is the only way to resolve it.
- No data on how well any cloud FBX retargets to `metahuman_base_skel` specifically.
