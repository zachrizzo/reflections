# Medieval London: cues for the playable city

These references describe late-medieval London or documented English buildings
of that period. They are design evidence for this game, not evidence that the
current map's modern plots or every new building reproduce a historical site.

- **Leadenhall:** the City of London's [Leadenhall report](https://democracy.cityoflondon.gov.uk/documents/g18120/Public%20reports%20pack%2021st-Mar-2017%2010.30%20Planning%20and%20Transportation%20Committee.pdf?T=10)
  describes a 15th-century grain store and market courtyard selling provisions.
  Give this district an open trading court, storage, sheltered stalls and a
  clear entrance; do not fill the court with a solid building.
- **Merchant halls:** the City's [medieval walk](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/assets/Things-to-do/medieval-city-self-guided-walk.pdf)
  describes livery halls with shops at the street and gateways to courtyards.
  A hall or occupied stronghold can have a distinctive gate and inner court;
  keep any passage and the existing player routes open and test them in game.
- **Market streets and river:** the City's [public-realm history](https://democracy.cityoflondon.gov.uk/documents/g17751/Public%20reports%20pack%2011th-Jan-2016%2011.30%20Streets%20and%20Walkways%20Sub%20Planning%20and%20Transportation%20Com.pdf?T=10)
  distinguishes markets and named trades from the wharfs and warehouses
  along the Thames. Vary the goods and street dressing by district rather
  than repeating identical stalls on every street.
- **Commercial frontages:** Historic England's [15th-century shop row](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1245908)
  records timber construction, individual shop bays, upper accommodation,
  a jettied floor and clay roof tiles. It is an English comparison, not a
  surviving London street. Use varied bay widths, shutters, timber and roof
  forms while keeping the surveyed building footprint and access clear.

Confirm every suggested court, gate, prop and landmark against the loaded
Gray London level, including nearby quests, collision and navigation, before
making it permanent.
