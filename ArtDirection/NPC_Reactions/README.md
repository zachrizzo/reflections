# NPC reaction prototype

The playable level `/Game/Characters/Hero/Sample/Lvl_HeroSampleCrowd` has six opt-in MetaHuman actors. During play, `UReflectionsNpcPopulationSubsystem` adds health, a reaction component, and a query-only hit proxy to each actor tagged `NPC_Reactive` and `NPC_HitProxy`. The Bridge Guard uses the guard disposition; the other five use timid, civilian, or brave dispositions. The two White London actors are tagged `Flesh`; one also opts into death ragdoll. The assets themselves were not modified for these behaviors.

Nearby world spell events are attenuated by distance and intensity. Timid NPCs can flee; brave and guard NPCs investigate; hostile NPCs or guards who distrust the player can approach and attack. Direct hits increase fear and hostility. Reactions publish `OnReactionChanged` for animation, UI, and dialogue systems. A runtime world reputation score ranges from -100 to 100; player spell hits on reactive NPCs subtract 8, and actual player healing of them adds up to 5. `AdjustReputation` allows other game systems to report deeds. This prototype does not persist reputation across level loads.

Validated in Play in Editor:

- A nearby timid NPC fled and moved; a nearby guard investigated and moved; a distant NPC remained calm.
- A fire hit reduced NPC health from 100 to 86 and player reputation from 0 to -8; a healing pulse restored that NPC to 100 and raised reputation to approximately -6.6. See `reputation-test.json`.
- At low reputation, a nearby guard entered the attacking state and the player's health fell during the test. See `guard-test.json`.
- An opt-in ragdoll test reduced a White London NPC from 5 health to 0, switched it to incapacitated, enabled body physics, and recorded nonzero body velocity. See `ragdoll-test.json`.

`Saved/Screenshots/WindowsEditor/CrowdLooks_QA.png` is an existing appearance preview, not a recording of these behavioral tests.

Nonlethal spell impacts now briefly simulate an upper-body physics branch and blend it back to the animated pose. The runtime selects a nearby arm or head physics body when the coarse hit proxy has no bone. Blast responses use a stronger outward/upward body impulse; actor-only NPC roots still move using a swept, horizontal kinematic knockback so they do not sink below the floor. In PIE, a fire bolt reduced a White London NPC from 100 to 86 health, produced measurable arm-body velocity, and returned the body to animation; a fireball reduced two nearby NPCs from 100 to 80, briefly simulated both, and returned the direct target to animation. Both actor origins remained at their original height in the sampled blast test. The measurements are in `Intermediate/NpcReactions/nonlethal-fire.json` and `nonlethal-blast.json`.

The reactions use simple direct movement and proximity attacks; obstacle-aware navigation, reaction and attack animation, tactical combat, and persistent reputation are not implemented. Nonlethal blasts are not full-body ragdoll flings: the body response is limited to an upper-body branch, and actor displacement remains kinematic. Only one White London actor is currently opted into death ragdoll. The capsule proxy is coarse and does not identify armor or individual hit bones; testing records physics state and recovery, not polished visual quality. The separate blood and fire effects are preliminary and do not establish unique finished visuals for all 20 spells. Legacy imported combat Blueprints still contain errors if opened independently, though the playable startup chain is isolated from them.

## Crowd movement and Blender refit

The crowd movement opt-in is `NPC_SampleLocomotion`. A clean Game Animation Sample CMC character drives each visible MetaHuman's transform and motion-matching pose. The retarget Blueprints under `/Game/Characters/CrowdMotion` target the five distinct body meshes in the test map. The visible actors retain their health, spell collision proxy, dialogue and hit physics. Calm actors walk a small route near their placed origin and resume ambient movement after their reaction expires. The routes are small local loops for the open test area and use CMC world collision; they do not provide city-scale navigation.

The Blender fitting script projects selected garment vertices toward the relevant body, maintains garment-specific ease, and transfers interpolated weights from the nearest body triangle. Coat tails, rag hems, footwear, props and the original UV and material topology are kept. Run the fitting script from this project with Blender in background mode. The output folder is `Intermediate/NpcMovement/Wardrobe`; pass `-- --verify-only` to validate the exported FBXs independently. These scripts use the existing stored fitting references rather than regenerating the outfits.

The fit checks compare the modeled body and garment in the rest pose and two synthetic strides. Nearest triangle normal flags decreased in all three poses for each of the 12 main garment pieces. A separate Blender pass shortened and narrowed the four Red NPC boot models against the existing target bodies, preserving their shafts, soles, UVs and weights. All four reduced possible inward flags in the rest measurement. These measurements screen for potential intersections. The Unreal import script keeps the current actor material overrides and leader pose relationships, and stores a pre-edit copy of the map in `Intermediate/NpcMovement`.

The fitting approach follows [Blender's Shrinkwrap guide](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.3/modeling/modifiers/deform/shrinkwrap.html) and [nearest-face Data Transfer mapping](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.3/modeling/modifiers/modify/data_transfer.html). The live body retargeting follows [Epic's Game Animation Sample MetaHuman guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/adding-a-metahuman-to-the-game-animation-sample-project-in-unreal-engine).

In the default map's combined PIE test, each of the six NPCs traveled 192–214 cm away from its starting sample over the observation window with the same `SandboxCharacter_CMC_ABP` source as the hero. The actual MetaHuman feet showed substantial local bone motion. The Coin Clerk's previous starting point intersected the traversal staircase. The map now places the Coin Clerk at (500, −150, 0), on open floor. The fire bolt damaged the visible White 01 NPC from 100 to 86, activated its hit physics, and recovered. All 16 NPC refits and five new hero garments were active. No test driver capsule penetrated static world geometry during 20 samples per NPC. Full measurements and close-up frames are in `Intermediate/NpcMovement/pie.json` and `Intermediate/NpcMovement/FinalCaptures`.

The fitted pieces cover the six main outfits, including coats, waistcoats, trousers, blouses, Red London boots and both White London tunics. The final captures show clipping between the Apprentice's coat and underlying layers, at the Clerk's shoulders, and where the Drummer's skirt overlaps the trousers. The White London rag hems are still bulky. Rag hems, White London sandals, and props remain separate fitting work, and garment clearance for the full animation library remains unverified.
