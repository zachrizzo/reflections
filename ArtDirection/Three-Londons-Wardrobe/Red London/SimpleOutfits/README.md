# Simple Red London outfits

The four Red London NPCs on the startup map now use a fitted tunic, trousers, and ankle boots. The detailed garment photos derive from the supplied Red London lineup and the three `*_Projection.glb` files are local Hunyuan3D-2 reconstructs. The tunic and trousers use the MetaHuman surface as their workable topology and project sampled relief from their own generated sources. The boots preserve the generated shoe silhouette after decimation and are shaped independently on all eight native feet. Garment color atlases use the generated front images and matching cloth crops or a mirrored projection on the reverse. Each native body uses its own normalizing skin weights and each tunic has a separate muted-red tint.

The Bridge Guard, Archive Apprentice, Coin Clerk and Festival Drummer use new Red London visual duplicates in `/Game/Characters/CrowdMotion/SimpleRed`. They preserve their faces, grooms, heights, reactive NPC tags and Game Animation Sample locomotion. Only the separate trimmed hands/neck shell is rendered for exposed body skin. They share the same simple garment designs with per-body fitting, and the original detailed clothing and props remain in their original Blueprints. The two White London rags actors retain the same blueprint and garment assignments.

PIE verified the new body/garment visibility, foot motion on all six NPCs, and a fire hit and physics recovery on White 01. Blender roundtrip checked the 16 FBXs for hierarchy, UVs and normalized weights. See `Intermediate/NpcMovement/pie.json` and `Intermediate/NpcMovement/SimpleRed/Captures`. The saved map has a backup before the wardrobe switch under `Intermediate/NpcMovement/SimpleRed`.

The source has a raised collar while the body-derived topology produces a lower, rounded neck opening. The shoe cuff projection and sole texture have some irregular shading at close range. These are shared prototypes with color variants. The play test covers short walks across the test map.
