# White London rune tattoos

Edits made with the built-in ImageGen tool using the three White London rag wardrobe lineups as references.

## white-01-survival-and-trade

Edit image 1, a four-person White London NPC costume lineup. Keep the original four adult characters in exactly the same order, head to toe, same faces, skin tones, untamed hair, poses, thin rag wraps, boots or sandals, accessories, and neutral photoreal studio lighting and framing. Change only the tattoos and their light. Replace ALL decorative tattoo patterns with a consistent fictional rune alphabet composed of discrete angular black-ink glyphs. Glyph shapes are built from two to five sharp straight ink strokes, such as a split-fork stem with an offset crossbar, a tall diamond bisected by a stroke, an interrupted triangle with a short upright, or a paired chevron with a single vertical. Each glyph is thumb-sized and separated by bare skin. Repeat glyph shapes in short sequences of three to six marks, readable as an inscription. Follow the curve of a collarbone, form a short vertical sentence down a forearm or shin, or use a few isolated marks beside a temple or across knuckles. Dense coverage overall, but clear spacing and contrast so the viewer can pick out the individual signs at this scale. Remove every decorative wave, linked zigzag band, scroll, flowing vine, illustration, continuous line, and dotwork pattern. No real-world letters, numerals, readable words, captions, or text in the background. Dark, aged ink lies flat within real skin texture. For each figure, one or two entire glyphs on a visible shoulder, hand, or neck emit subtle pale violet light precisely inside the ink strokes. Every other glyph is dark. No floating symbols or effects. Believable photo of the same wardrobe and same people, with this new tattoo script.
Use the same alphabet for all four. Vary the layout from left to right: diagonals on shoulder and shin, stacked forearm columns, spaced rows around an upper arm, and small tightly arranged glyphs across the collar and down the calves.
