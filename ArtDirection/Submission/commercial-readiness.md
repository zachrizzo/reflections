# Commercial readiness: can *Reflections* be sold as it stands?

The orchestrator asked for this on 2026-11-14 (around 12:40 MST), after the team said it may sell the game after the jam. It's read-only research: no files changed outside this folder. Sources are linked at the bottom. **This isn't legal advice.** Before selling, get a lawyer to read the four **NO** / **UNCLEAR** items marked ⚠.

**Bottom line:** most of the game is cleared for a commercial Unreal Engine release. **Two things must be redone before selling:** the 6 ElevenLabs cues (generated on the free tier) and the Hunyuan3D-2 garment meshes (their licence excludes the EU, UK and South Korea). Four things need a decision or a check: where the blood effect came from, whether Advanced Locomotion V4 ships, how close the premise still is to the books, and a trademark search on the title.

## 3. IP originality after the rename

| Element | Status now | Commercial use | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World, coin and character **names**; "London"; title | Renamed everywhere players see or hear (rename table done, all owners checked) | **OK** | None |
| ⚠ **Shard rule W32** (`DA_WorldJumpConfig` → Coin Rule = `destination`) | Still "a shard **of the side you're entering**", the books' signature mechanic (a token from the destination world) | **UNCLEAR**: the combination is the risk, not any one element | **Set Coin Rule to `current` or `any`.** It's a one-field config change for world-jump. The on-screen tip and narration are already rule-neutral. |
| Three versions of one city: dull, glowing and cracked/cold | Names and descriptions changed, and the sides are no longer described by magic level or rulers. The colour scheme (gray / red-gold / white) and world roles still follow the books. | **UNCLEAR** (ideas aren't protected, but structure plus look plus mechanic add up) | Before selling: give each side a distinct identity that isn't magic level (Pewterside as a mirror-industry town, Frostside as frozen time, and so on), and drop the dull/vivid/brutal hierarchy |
| "The Silverer", one of the few who can walk between | Original name, a mirror-craft framing | **OK-ish** (the rare-traveller idea is generic) | Keep the mirror-craft angle prominent |
| Villain that possesses people | "The Tarnish", spreading through reflections | **OK** (possession is a common trope; the expression is original) | None |
| Hero's coat that changes per side | A runtime tint, not a reversible coat with many sides | **OK**, but the concept art is a problem: `ArtDirection/Three-Londons-Wardrobe/prompts.md:108` asks for a coat "inspired by Kell's … coat from A Darker Shade of Magic" | Never publish that prompt or its images. The in-game coat is a separately built mesh. |
| ⚠ **Title "Reflections"** and **"Lanthorne"** | Not searched | **UNCLEAR**: "Reflections" is a common word and other games use it, so it would be hard to register and could conflict | Run a USPTO/EUIPO trademark search before buying store pages or marketing. Consider a more distinctive commercial title. |

**Recommended before any sale:** set W32 to `current` or `any`, differentiate the three sides, and have an IP lawyer compare the final game against *Shades of Magic*. Removing the names was necessary, but it may not be enough on its own for a commercial product.
