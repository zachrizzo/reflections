# Canon sheet: names, pronunciation and voice lines

These are the names the team chose on 2026-11-14 ([originality.md](originality.md), set B). Use this sheet for any text or voice in *Reflections*. The audio session generates the character voices: **Google Gemini** designed voices for the people, Wren and the story, and **OpenAI** text-to-speech for the Tarnish and the narrator. Live NPC replies, when a Gemini key is present, are spoken in the same Gemini voices. Players must be told the voices are AI-generated (OpenAI's usage policies require it), and the credits and the first-talk notice already say so ([ai-declaration.md](ai-declaration.md)).

## Never say

- **London**, "Three Londons", Grey, Red, White or Black London, Kell, Lila, Holland, Rhy, Osaron, Antari, "As Travars", blood magic, or "traveller" for Wren. The old premise followed V.E. Schwab's *Shades of Magic*, and the jam's rules ban others' IP.
- Don't describe the sides by **how much magic they have** or **who rules them** ("Pewterside has no magic", "a wasteland ruled by…"). That restates the books' set-up. Describe **look and mood** instead: dull, glowing, cracked, cold.
- Real brands or real places. Everything is in English.
- On 2026-11-14 the team chose an **M rating** and accepted the jam risk ([README.md](README.md), decision 2b), so content isn't limited to Teen any more. Mature themes are a creative choice now, not a rule.

## Voice lines (a starter script; trim to what the game triggers)

Keep each line under about 3 seconds. Wren sounds warm, steady and a little wry. The Tarnish whispers, close to the mic, and is amused. Citizens are short, scared and natural.
