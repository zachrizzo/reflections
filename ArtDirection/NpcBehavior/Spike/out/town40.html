A town of forty, twenty-five years

Each town starts with about 26 founders: couples, widows with children, siblings sharing a house. Over 25 years, month by month, children are born and grow up, take apprenticeships, fall in love, inherit shops, debts and grudges. Each town also gets its own temperament, and a director that leaves out about a third of the kinds of trouble. Every event remembers its cause.

How samey are the towns?

A story shape is three events in a row along a chain of causes, such as theft → blame → fight. For each version of the rules, 100 towns were generated (1,000 for the final rules) and every pair compared. Overlap is the share of story shapes two towns have in common: lower means each new game tells more of its own stories.

Is it robust?
